# Operational Summary

Official updated text of Argentina's Ley 27.506, which creates the Régimen de Promoción de la Economía del Conocimiento. The source applies nationally and should be treated as legal source material for software, digital services, promoted professional services exports, R&D, training, registration, benefits, revalidation, and sanctions.

This summary is for internal demo retrieval only. It is not legal advice. External use requires review by the Andén legal team.

## Demo-Relevant Notes

- Promoted activities include software and digital services, audiovisual production, biotech, professional services exports, nanotechnology, aerospace, nuclear engineering, and industry 4.0 solutions.
- Companies generally need to show that a substantial share of revenue comes from promoted activities and must satisfy additional requirements such as quality, training/R&D investment, or exports.
- The law establishes a national beneficiary registry and recurring revalidation duties.
