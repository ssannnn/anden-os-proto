# Operational Summary

Official text of Argentina's Ley 24.331 on free zones. The law defines the free-zone framework, objectives, activities, provincial adherence, authorities, and fiscal/customs treatment.

For Andén OS, this source is useful mostly as a boundary document. The demo should avoid implying that Andén's "digital zone" concept has customs or tax treatment equivalent to an Argentine free zone unless legal validates that statement.

## Demo-Relevant Notes

- Free zones are tied to customs territory concepts and export-oriented operations.
- Provincial implementation and authority details matter for any serious analysis.
- Use this source in government stakeholder briefings when questions involve "zona franca" comparisons.
