# Operational Summary

Mock FAQ for onboarding companies into the Digital Zone pipeline. The document defines the operational flow, document request categories, fit assessment, AI summary, legal routing, and next step assignment.

## Company Intake Checklist

- Create company profile.
- Request incorporation and tax documents.
- Request product, revenue, and sector description.
- Request compliance and regulatory context where relevant.
- Generate AI summary and AI recommended action.
- Route legal or regulated claims to legal review.
