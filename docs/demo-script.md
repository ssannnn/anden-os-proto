# 7-Minute Demo Script

Audience: Anden founder, operations, partnerships, or legal stakeholder.

Positioning line:

> We are not showing a concept deck. This is a first version of the internal
> operating system Anden could use to run knowledge, partners, companies,
> documents, workflows, and executive decisions with AI.

## 0:00-0:45 - Login And Frame

1. Open the deployed URL.
2. Enter the demo access code.
3. Land on `/dashboard`.

Say:

- This is an internal backoffice, not a marketing site.
- The password gate makes it shareable without full account setup.
- Business data is mocked; Argentina regulatory sources are curated real
  sources.
- The demo is light-theme only and supports EN/ES from the top-right toggle.

## 0:45-2:00 - Executive Dashboard

Show:

- companies tracked
- institutional partners
- documents indexed
- pending workflows
- AI retrieval confidence
- hours saved this week
- AI spend cap: `$0.00 / $5.00`

Say:

- The first screen gives an operating pulse.
- The founder can see pipeline, alerts, active workflows, and AI activity in one
  place.
- The cost guard is visible because this is a public demo that must stay cheap.

## 2:00-3:15 - AI Knowledge Assistant

Open `/assistant`.

Run two prompts:

1. `What is Anden's value proposition?`
2. `Summarize the requirements for a company to join an Argentina digital zone.`

Show:

- cited sources
- original-language label for Spanish regulatory sources
- operational inference
- legal review warning

Say:

- The assistant is not just chat. It is source-backed internal knowledge.
- Legal-sensitive regulatory interpretation is explicitly flagged for review.

## 3:15-4:10 - CRM And Partner Context

Open `/companies`, then `AtlasPay`.

Show:

- sector, country, status, priority
- AI summary
- AI recommended action
- linked documents

Then open `/partners` or mention the partner relevance card.

Say:

- The CRM is AI-native: records are not just fields, they include recommended
  next actions and document context.
- Partners become part of operational decisioning, especially for fintech
  companies.

## 4:10-5:15 - Workflow Automation

Open `/workflows`.

Run:

1. `Company onboarding`
2. Optional if time allows: `Prepare meeting`

Show:

- visible step progression
- generated outputs
- citations
- legal review flag
- run history

Say:

- These are repeatable internal processes, not one-off prompts.
- The system can prepare documents, fit assessment, meeting briefs, risks, and
  follow-ups from the same knowledge layer.

## 5:15-6:25 - Weekly Operating Brief

Return to `/dashboard`.

Click `Generate Weekly Operating Brief`.

Show:

- provider/model and estimated cost
- progress
- risks
- blockers
- opportunities
- recommended next actions
- legal review items
- source citations

Then open `/reports`.

Say:

- This is the founder-facing payoff: automatic reporting from the operating
  system.
- Generated reports are saved with locale, provider/model, cost, citations, and
  legal review state.

## 6:25-7:00 - Close

Say:

- The value is less manual coordination, faster onboarding, centralized
  knowledge, automatic reporting, and better legal review discipline.
- The next production step is connecting real Anden operational data and
  hardening auth/permissions.

Final line:

> This should exist inside the company because it gives the team leverage
> immediately, even before becoming a full production system.
